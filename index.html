Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026

LearningLoop provides CozyCornerColoringBook (the “App”). This policy explains how information is handled when you use the App, contact us, or visit this privacy policy page.

The App does not collect or upload personal information. It has no advertisements, analytics or crash-reporting services, user accounts, or in-app purchases.

Information in the App

We do not collect personal information through the App or receive your coloring activity, artwork, device identifiers, or usage information. The App does not upload your data to us.

Any coloring work or settings retained locally by the App remain on your device and are not accessible to us. Device-level backup features, if enabled, are controlled by your device settings and backup provider.

Advertising, tracking, and sharing

The App does not display ads or use analytics or crash-reporting services. It does not require an account or offer in-app purchases. We do not sell, rent, or share personal information collected through the App because the App does not collect it.

When you contact us

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address and the information you include in your message. We use this information to respond to your inquiry and handle your support or privacy request. Please do not send unnecessary personal information, including information about children.

Our contact address uses Gmail, so correspondence is processed by Google to provide email services under its Privacy Policy. We do not use support correspondence for advertising or sell it.

Retention and deletion

We do not retain App user data on our servers because the App does not send us that data. You can remove any locally stored App data using Android’s app storage settings or by uninstalling the App. Files saved outside the App and device backups, if any, must be managed separately through your device or backup provider.

We retain support correspondence only as long as needed to resolve your request and related follow-up, unless a longer retention period is required by law. To request deletion of correspondence you sent us, email robertchaneyjr71@gmail.com. There are no App accounts to delete.

Children’s privacy

The App’s no-collection practices apply to all users, including children. The App does not collect personal information from children or display advertising. A parent or guardian with questions about privacy, or who believes a child has sent personal information to us by email, may contact us to request its deletion.

Security

Keeping App information on the device limits the information disclosed to us. Please protect your device using its available security controls. Support correspondence is handled through our email service; no electronic communication or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

This privacy policy website

This page contains no advertising, analytics scripts, external fonts, or contact forms. When published on GitHub Pages, GitHub processes visitor information, including IP addresses for security purposes, as described in its Privacy Statement. This website hosting is separate from the App’s data practices.

Changes to this policy

We will post changes on this page and update the effective date. If the App’s data practices change, we will update this policy and provide any notice or consent required before introducing those changes.
